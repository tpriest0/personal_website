Research

Eco-evolutionary dynamics of microbial life

I am a microbial ecologist studying the diversification, adaptation, and ecological structuring of microbes across ecological and environmental gradients. My research began by combining fieldwork, molecular techniques, and bioinformatics to address these questions from the ground up; more recently, it has evolved toward global-scale metagenomic analysis combined with new computational frameworks and advanced statistical approaches to resolve and rigorously test biological signal beyond the reach of current methods.

Patterns

Microbial community dynamics and sub-species diversification — how diversity is organised in nature, from whole communities down to genomically discrete populations.

Drivers

The dynamics of mobile genetic elements — a major engine of microbial adaptation and evolutionary change.

Guiding questions
  1. 01 What is the diversity of microbial life across Earth's ecosystems, and what processes structure it over space and time?
  2. 02 What mechanisms enable microbes to adapt and diversify across different ecological contexts?
  3. 03 How do ecological and evolutionary processes interact to shape the diversification of microbial life?
  4. 04 How do mobile genetic elements drive adaptation and ecological diversification, and shape the eco-evolutionary consequences of environmental change?
